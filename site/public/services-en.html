Would you like your existing MRI or CT images assessed independently, and the results explained to you? Radioloq provides a written medical second opinion and, if you wish, a personal video consultation in the language offered.

The options

Basic 50 euros

You receive a written, reasoned medical second assessment of your existing examination, addressing your specific medical question. The report contains the medical assessment together with an explanation of the results in plain language.

Included are the assessment of one current examination of one body region and a written report as a PDF of two pages. A video consultation is not included.

Standard 84 euros

After reviewing your images, we first discuss your case in a video appointment of around 15 minutes. What you tell me there about your history and your symptoms then goes into the written assessment.

You then receive a written, reasoned second assessment. At the end of the report there is a section: what does this mean for me? There I set out what the findings mean and name the points you should clarify with the physician treating you.

Included are the video consultation, the assessment of one current examination of one body region, the accompanying medical documents and the written report as a PDF of two pages.

Premium 158 euros

Two examinations are assessed, one of them a previous examination of the same body region. We assess the changes that can be identified over time and what they mean for your medical question.

Here too we discuss your case first, in a video appointment of around 30 minutes. Both assessments are then set out in one single document of four pages, which also closes with the same section: what does this mean for me?

This option presupposes that a previous examination of the same body region exists. If there is none, Standard is the better fit.

Included are the video consultation, the current examination and one previous examination of the same body region, the accompanying medical documents and the written report as a PDF of four pages.

Booking and payment

You select your option, submit your documents and pay the amount shown online. Your booking and payment are confirmed automatically by email.

Before any medical work begins, we check whether your documents and your question are suitable for the option you selected. If the service cannot properly be delivered within the option as described, we will tell you. No additional costs arise without your agreement. If the assignment is therefore not carried out, you receive a full refund.

Billing and reimbursement

Medical services are billed in accordance with the German scale of fees for physicians (Gebührenordnung für Ärzte, GOÄ). After the service has been provided you receive an invoice listing the services rendered, the fee items and the multipliers applied. The amount you have already paid is credited against it. Service components that were not provided or are not billable are not charged. The corresponding amounts are refunded.

Our services are self-pay services. We cannot guarantee reimbursement by your health insurer or by a public-sector assistance scheme.

Medical notice

The second opinion is based on the documents you provide. It does not replace a necessary in-person examination. Please also discuss the results and the next steps with the physician treating you.
