2. What Data We Collect

We collect only data necessary for providing the service (principle of data minimization, Art. 5(1)(c) GDPR):

2.1 Data provided directly by you

  • Identity data: Your name
  • Contact data: Your email address
  • Medical image files: MRI, CT, X-ray, or other diagnostic imaging you submit (these are special categories of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR)
  • Clinical context: Symptom descriptions, prior diagnoses, or imaging dates you voluntarily provide
  • Language preference: The language in which you wish to receive your evaluation
  • Payment data: Payments are processed entirely via Stripe or PayPal. We do not receive or store card numbers, bank details, or full payment information

2.2 Automatically collected data

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and basic access logs retained by the web hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare) for security purposes
  • We use technically necessary cookies and, only with your consent, Google Analytics for traffic measurement
  • We do not use tracking pixels, behavioral analytics, or advertising cookies
  • Google Analytics is only loaded after your explicit consent via our cookie banner

6. Medical Image Data: Special Safeguards

Your medical image files are special categories of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR. We handle this data with the utmost care:

  • Files are used solely to prepare your evaluation and are not used for any other purpose
  • Files are stored only as long as necessary to provide the service (see Section 7)
  • Files are not used for training AI systems, machine learning, or automated decision-making
  • Files are not shared with research institutions, healthcare providers, or commercial entities
  • Access to your files is restricted exclusively to Adalat Ganjali as the service provider

8. Your Rights (Art. 13(2)(b)–(e) GDPR)

Under GDPR and BDSG, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15): You may request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): You may request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 / "right to be forgotten"): You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18): Under certain circumstances, you may request that we restrict processing of your data
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): You may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object (Art. 21): You may object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): You may withdraw your consent to processing of your medical image data at any time, without giving reasons and with effect for the future, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with the competent supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, please contact info@radioloq.com. We respond within 30 days in accordance with Art. 12 GDPR.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that would have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR). All evaluations are performed by a human.

12. Data Security (Art. 32 GDPR)

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL (HTTPS)
  • Access to submitted files is restricted exclusively to the service provider
  • Payment data is processed exclusively by PCI-DSS certified payment service providers
  • We do not store card numbers, CVV codes, or bank details

In the event of a data breach affecting your rights and freedoms, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours (Art. 33 GDPR) and inform affected users without undue delay where required (Art. 34 GDPR).

13. Cookies and Tracking

Radioloq uses technically necessary cookies for basic session functions. These do not require consent under § 25(2) TTDSG.

Additionally, with your explicit consent, we use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited) for anonymized traffic measurement. On your first visit to the website, you will see a cookie banner through which you can accept or decline the use of Google Analytics. Your choice is stored locally in your browser. Google Analytics is only loaded after consent is given. If declined, no data is transmitted to Google. You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting locally stored browser data and reloading the page.

Payment service providers (Stripe, PayPal) may set their own cookies on their respective payment pages in accordance with their own privacy policies.

14. Automatic Language Detection

On your first visit, Radioloq automatically detects your country of origin based on the country header provided by Cloudflare (CF-IPCountry). This information is used to display the website in your country's language (e.g., Turkish for visitors from Turkey). The IP address itself is neither stored nor processed by Radioloq. Country detection occurs exclusively at Cloudflare's network edge. You may manually change your language selection at any time using the language selector buttons. Your selection is stored in your browser (localStorage) and preserved for future visits. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in language accessibility).

15. Children's Data

This service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 without verifiable parental consent under Art. 8 GDPR. If you believe a child's data has been submitted without appropriate authorization, please contact us immediately at info@radioloq.com and we will delete the data without delay.
